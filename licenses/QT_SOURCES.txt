dans _internal/PySide6 et/ou _internal/shiboken6 par une construction Windows x64
compatible avec l'ABI Qt/PySide 6.11 et le Python 3.14 embarqué. Remplacer ensemble
les fichiers interdépendants. Relancer ThermalCurve.exe et vérifier vos résultats.
Aucun contrôle de signature propre à ThermalCurve n'interdit ce remplacement.

modifications restent applicables. La licence PolyForm ne les restreint pas.

EN
This application uses Qt, PySide and Shiboken under LGPL v3.
Copyright © The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors.
GPL v3 and LGPL v3 texts are retained in the directories identified above.
DLLs/extensions come from installed PySide6/Shiboken6 6.11.1 packages, without
code modifications. Corresponding official source archives are provided separately
as ThermalCurve-1.0.0-third-party-sources.zip. SOURCE_ARCHIVES_SHA256.txt records
their hashes. Preserve access to these sources when redistributing the application.

To replace a library, close ThermalCurve and copy the whole application folder
as a backup. Replace the relevant DLLs/extensions in _internal/PySide6 and/or
_internal/shiboken6 with a Windows x64 build compatible with the Qt/PySide 6.11 ABI
and bundled Python 3.14. Replace interdependent files together. Restart
ThermalCurve.exe and check your results. ThermalCurve has no custom signature
check preventing replacement. LGPL rights to modify and reverse engineer to
debug these changes remain applicable and are not restricted by PolyForm.
